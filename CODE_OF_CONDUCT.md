# Code of conduct

Mission Builds HQ is for builders of every age, including kids. Everyone here, in issues, pull requests, and comments, agrees to:

- **Be kind.** Everyone was a beginner once. No put-downs, insults, or "that's easy."
- **Keep it clean.** No swearing, mature content, or anything you wouldn't say in a classroom.
- **Keep personal info private.** Never post your full name, address, school, phone number, or photos of people without their permission. Kids: ask a grown-up before you post.
- **Help, don't just judge.** If code is wrong, explain why and how to fix it.
- **Stay on topic.** Robots, code, builds, space, and making things.

Posts that break these rules are edited or removed, and repeat problems lead to a block. To report something privately, use the "Report content" option on the post on GitHub.
